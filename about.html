About LearnSphere

Knowledge Without Borders.

LearnSphere helps learners find books, study materials, courses and universities in one place, starting with Kenya and growing to the world.

What you can do here

  • Explore subjects across school, university, technology, business and life skills.
  • Search millions of books through Open Library and a growing Kenyan collection.
  • Browse Kenyan universities and the courses they offer.
  • Save books to your own private library.

How we handle content

We do not host copyrighted books without permission. Every resource shows its source and licence, and we link to the owner wherever the material is not openly available.

Built in the open, step by step

LearnSphere is a student-built project. If you notice a mistake or want to contribute materials, please get in touch.
