Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what LearnSphere collects, why, and your choices. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Your activity: books you save and, in future, your quiz results and learning progress.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address and browser type, kept by our hosting and database providers for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show your saved books and progress to you, and only to you.
  • To protect the platform from abuse and fix problems.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show advertising to learners.
  • We do not make student profiles public.

Services that process data for us

LearnSphere uses Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (hosting). When you search the international library, your browser contacts Open Library directly, and your search words and IP address are visible to them under their own policy. Links to external sites are governed by those sites' policies.

Children and teenagers

LearnSphere is meant to be safe for learners of all ages. If you are under 18, please use the platform with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians can contact us to ask for a child's account to be reviewed or deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Email us at the address on the Contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time. We handle personal data in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Security

Your data is stored behind row-level access rules so that each person can only read their own records. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
