Frequently asked questions

Is LearnSphere free?

Yes. Searching, exploring and saving books are free. Optional paid features may be added later, and the free resources will stay free.

Does LearnSphere contain every book in the world?

No single site can. We connect you to millions of books through Open Library and add Kenyan materials with permission. Each resource shows where it comes from and how you can read or borrow it.

Can I download KCSE past papers and set books?

Only where the owner allows it. Many past papers and set books are copyrighted, so we link to the legitimate source instead of copying them.

How accurate is the university and course list?

It is built from official sources and is still growing. Always confirm accreditation with the Commission for University Education and the university before applying.

How do I save a book?

Create a free account, log in, search the library and tap Save. Your saved books appear on your dashboard and are visible only to you.

I did not get my confirmation email.

Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. If it still does not arrive, try registering again or contact us.

How can I contribute materials?

If you are a teacher or author and own the rights to your material, contact us. Contributions are reviewed before they are published.
